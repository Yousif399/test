Dr. Karam Ashoo

Dentist

Dr. Ashoo has been practicing endodontics in the area since 2009. Originally from Mississauga, he attended Columbia University in New York City to attain his dental degree, returning there three years later to complete his specialty training in Endodontics. Dr. Ashoo is committed to providing ethical, expert care with an experience that is both personalized and comfortable. He adopts the latest technology in his diagnosis and treatment, practicing endodontics at the highest standard.


Dr. Richard F. Hunter

Dentist

As many of you have already heard, Dr. Richard Hunter, who established this office way back in July of 1980, has retired. Richard is passionate about dentistry and will do his best to explain any treatment you may require, as well as alternative treatment options and what prognosis you can expect. Richard has lived and worked in the North Toronto community since returning from graduate school. In 1980 he established (his) this practice, limited to endodontics, in the Yonge and Davisville area. Richard and his wife Judi have raised 3 lovely daughters one of which has chosen dentistry as her career.


Dr. Alice Li

Dentist

Dr. Li obtained her Ph.D. and M.Sc. (Endodontics) from the University of Toronto. She grew up in Taiwan and practiced as an endodontist in teaching hospitals before moving to Canada. Dr. Li is warm, gentle and passionate. She takes great pride in addressing her patients' needs. She is committed to providing evidence-based treatments for her patients. Dr. Li received numerous national and international awards and accolades for her contributions to the field of endodontics. Her research has been published in the Journal of Endodontics and the International Endodontic Journal. Dr. Li remains closely associated with the Faculty of Dentistry at the University of Toronto, both as a clinical instructor in Endodontics, and she continues her award winning research . During her free time Dr. Li enjoys sitting at her favorite coffee shop, practicing piano and staying active with the latest fad in-home workouts.


Dr. Michael Tiedemann

System Analyst

Michael was born and raised in Sudbury, Ontario. He completed his BSc and Ph.D. in chemistry at Western University, where he also earned his dental degree in 2017. Michael finished as class president and won the Endodontic Award. For the next three years he practiced dentistry privately in Hamilton and Grimsby, Ontario, and returned to academia at the University of Toronto for his specialty training in Endodontics. He received his MSc from the University of Toronto and his Fellowship (Royal College of Dentists of Canada) in Endodontics in 2022.
